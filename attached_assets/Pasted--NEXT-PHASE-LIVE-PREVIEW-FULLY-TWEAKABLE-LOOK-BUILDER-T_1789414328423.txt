# 17. DCP COMPONENT BLENDING

Be careful with DCP behavior.

Do not blindly interpolate every field in a mathematically invalid way.

For each type of component, define a proper strategy.

Examples:

Matrices:
interpolate toward identity where appropriate for strength.

Tone curves:
interpolate curve output.

HueSat tables:
interpolate deltas.

LookTables:
interpolate output transform.

LUTs:
interpolate transformed RGB with original RGB.

If a component cannot be safely blended:

show:

FIXED / NON-BLENDABLE

rather than inventing math.
